# Account & Profile

Your MCCompanion account ties together your app, your Minecraft identities, and your social presence.

---

## Creating an account

You can create an account in two ways:

- **In the app:** Download MCCompanion on Android, iOS, Windows, or macOS and follow the on-screen registration.
- **On the website:** Go to [mccompanion.net/register](/register) to register directly from your browser.

### Username rules

- **3 to 20 characters**
- Lowercase letters (a–z), digits (0–9), and underscores only
- No spaces or special characters
- Must be unique — usernames cannot be changed after registration

---

## Editing your profile

Open the **My Account** tab in the dashboard or your profile screen in the app:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Display name** | Optional friendly name shown alongside your username (max 32 chars) |
| **Bio** | Short description shown on your public profile (max 200 chars) |
| **Avatar** | Set via the app, upload any image as your profile picture |
| **Appear offline** | Hides your online status from other users |

---

## Linking Minecraft accounts

Linking your Minecraft accounts lets other players find you and unlocks the relay modes.

### Xbox / Bedrock
1. Open the app and go to **Profile → Link Xbox Account**
2. Follow the Microsoft device-code flow. Sign in with your Microsoft account.
3. Your gamertag and XUID are stored and shown on your profile.

### Java Edition
1. Open the app and go to **Profile → Link Java Account**
2. Follow the Minecraft device-code flow. Sign in with your Mojang/Microsoft account.
3. Your Java username and UUID are stored and linked.

You can link multiple accounts of each type. Linked accounts are visible on your public profile and are used by the player lookup feature.

:::info
Account linking happens in the app only. You can view linked accounts on the website dashboard, but cannot add or remove them there.
:::

## Subscriptions and tracker slots

The number of servers you can track depends on your subscription:

| Plan | Tracker slots |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 |
| Starter | 3 |
| Pro | 6 |
| Ultra | 10 |

---

## Roles

Your account can have additional roles beyond the default **user** role:

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| `user` | Every registered account |
| `partner` | Server owners listed in the Featured Servers program |
| `admin` | MCCompanion staff |

Roles are granted by an admin and visible in the dashboard.

---

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the app settings. This permanently removes all your data including profile, friends, messages, and tracked servers.

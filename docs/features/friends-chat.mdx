# Friends & Chat

MCCompanion has a built-in social layer: add other players as friends, see when they are online, and chat directly inside the app.

---

## Adding friends

1. Go to **Profile → Friends** or use Player Lookup to find a user
2. Tap **Add friend** on their profile
3. They will receive a friend request notification
4. Once they accept, they appear in your friends list

---

## Friend requests

- Incoming requests appear under **Friends → Requests**
- Tap **Accept** to confirm or **Decline** to ignore
- Declined requests are not shown to the sender

---

## Online status

Friends who are currently active in the app show a green indicator on their avatar. You can hide your own status by enabling **Appear offline** in your profile settings.

---

## Chat

You can send direct messages to any of your friends:

1. Open your **Friends** list and tap a friend
2. Tap **Message** to open the conversation
3. Type your message and tap **Send**

Messages are delivered in real time. You will receive a push notification when you get a new message (if notifications are enabled).

:::info
Chat is only available between mutual friends. Both sides must have accepted the friend request before messaging is possible.
:::

---

## Blocking users

1. Go to their profile
2. Tap **Block**
3. They can no longer send you friend requests or messages, and you will not appear in their searches

Blocked users can be managed under **Profile → Blocked users**.

---

## Reporting users

1. Open the user's profile or a message from them
2. Tap **Report**
3. Select a reason: **Spam**, **Harassment**, **Inappropriate content**, or **Other**
4. Optionally add extra context and submit

Reports are reviewed by MCCompanion moderators. Repeated violations can result in chat restrictions or a full account ban.

---

## Removing a friend

Go to your **Friends** list, tap the friend, and select **Remove friend**. This removes them from both sides without sending a notification.

# Skins

MCCompanion includes a full skin manager. Browse thousands of community skins, upload your own, preview them in 3D, and apply them to your Bedrock account.

---

## Browsing skins

Open the **Skins** tab in the app to browse the skin library. You can:
- Scroll through popular and recently added skins
- Search by name or category
- Preview any skin in 3D before saving

---

## Applying a skin

1. Tap any skin to open its preview
2. Tap **Apply** to set it as your active Minecraft skin
3. The skin is applied to your linked Bedrock (Xbox) account via the Geyser skin system

:::info
Applying skins requires a linked Xbox/Bedrock account. See [Account & Profile](/docs/features/account) for how to link one.
:::

---

## Uploading a custom skin

1. Go to **Skins → Upload**
2. Select a `.png` file from your device (64×64 or 128×128 pixels)
3. Choose the skin model: **Classic** (4-pixel arms) or **Slim** (3-pixel arms)
4. Tap **Upload**. The skin is saved to your library

Custom skins are stored in your personal skin library and can be applied at any time.

---

## Skin editor

The built-in skin editor lets you modify any skin pixel by pixel:

- Tap **Edit** on any skin in your library
- Use the color picker and brush tools to paint
- Toggle between body parts: head, body, arms, legs, and outer layers
- Preview changes in the live 3D viewer
- Tap **Save** when done. The edited skin is saved as a new entry in your library

---

## Skin library

Your saved skins are stored in the app under **My Skins**. From there you can:
- Rename skins
- Re-apply a previously used skin
- Delete skins you no longer need

---

## Tips

- Skins are 64×64 pixels (classic format) or 128×128 (HD, Bedrock compatible)
- Transparent pixels are supported for hair, accessories, and overlays
- Java and Bedrock use slightly different formats. MCCompanion handles the conversion automatically

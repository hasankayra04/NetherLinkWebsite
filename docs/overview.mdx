# MCCompanion Docs

Welcome to the MCCompanion documentation. Here you will find everything you need to get connected, discover features, and get the most out of the app.

---

## What is MCCompanion?

MCCompanion is an all-in-one Minecraft companion app that lets you join **any Minecraft server from your console**, look up players, manage skins, track servers, and stay connected with friends, all from one place.

Available on **Windows**, **macOS**, **Android**, and **iOS**.

The app is available in **16 languages**: English, Arabic, Bengali, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian, Swahili, Turkish, Urdu, and Chinese.
